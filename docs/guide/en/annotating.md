# Dimensions and text

[Guide](../../../README.en.md#documentation) · [Français](../annoter.md)

![Linear dimensions of the facade and roof, leader texts on the roof and the terrace](../../media/cotes.jpg)

- The **Dimension** tool measures a length between two points or along an edge; the value
  follows the model's unit and precision. Radial dimensions in SketchUp files are read and
  written.
- The **Text** tool places a leader text: on a face it shows its area by default; on an edge,
  its length; on a component, its name. Without a leader, it stays fixed on screen.
- **3D Text** creates text as a volume (or as outlines), in a system font.
- Attached dimensions and texts follow what they are attached to: move, rotate or scale the
  wall and the dimension follows. These attachments are read and written in the `.skp`.
- **Fenêtre › Infos sur le modèle › Cotations** and **Texte** (Window › Model Info ›
  Dimensions, Text) set font, arrows and alignment; **Entity Info** edits a selected dimension or
  text.
- **Outils › Mètre** (Tools › Tape Measure) measures a distance and places guides; the
  **Protractor** places angle guides. **Édition › Supprimer les guides** (Edit › Delete Guides)
  removes them.
